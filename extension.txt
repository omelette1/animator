Describe your extension(s) here before implementing them.
My extension adds a collection of built-in geometric shapes to the animator, including circles, squares, rectangles, and ellipses. Users can specify each shape’s position and dimensions in their drawing code, then animate its outline using the existing path timing and thickness controls. Dimensions can depend on the shape slider, allowing users to resize multiple shapes interactively and create patterns that expand or shrink together. I will also add an original example drawing that combines these shapes into a customizable robot, with the slider changing its proportions. The existing straight-line drawings, playback controls, and saved drawing library will continue to work.
