My extension adds a collection of built-in geometric shapes to the Animator, including circles, squares, rectangles, and ellipses. Users can specify each shape's position and dimensions in their drawing code, then animate its outline using the existing path timing and thickness controls. Dimensions can depend on the shape slider, allowing users to resize shapes interactively and create patterns that expand or shrink.

To demonstrate my extension, I created an original turtle drawing that combines these geometric shapes. The turtle uses ellipses for its shell and flippers, circles for its head and eye, and lines for its tail, shell decorations, and smile. The shape slider changes the width of the turtle's shell, allowing users to customize its appearance. The extension preserves the existing straight-line drawings, playback controls, and saved drawing library.
